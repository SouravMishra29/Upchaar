Definition:
Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature, usually due to infection.

Common Causes:
- Viral infections (most common)
- Bacterial infections
- Heat exhaustion
- Inflammatory conditions

Risk Factors:
- Age (children, elderly)
- Chronic diseases (diabetes, heart disease)
- Weak immune system

Key Symptoms:
- Elevated temperature (>100.4°F)
- Chills
- Sweating
- Headache
- Muscle aches

Red Flags (Seek Immediate Care):
- Fever >103°F
- Lasting more than 3 days
- Difficulty breathing
- Confusion
- Persistent vomiting

General Care:
- Hydration
- Rest
- Monitor temperature regularly
